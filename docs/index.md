# Hibike

Your music. Your rhythm. An open-source music player for desktop and Android, maintained by Flugel.

Hibike plays local audio and offers online music search and playback, an adaptive pure-black interface, favorites, a playback queue and system media controls. Features vary by platform and build. The project is under active development; experimental builds may be unstable.

## Optional Google connection

Builds with Google sign-in can read your YouTube playlists and liked videos after you authorize access. Hibike requests the read-only `youtube.readonly` permission to display your library. It does not use this permission to upload videos, post comments or modify your account. Google credentials are not provided to yt-dlp for playback.

Google sign-in is optional. Local playback does not require connecting a Google account. You can revoke authorization through [Google account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
